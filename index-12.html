Skin education

Know your skin

Short, honest reads to help you decide.

How to build a simple routine

Read more

Morning: cleanse, moisturise, sunscreen. Evening: cleanse, treat, moisturise. Add one new product at a time, and patch-test first.

Why your skin feels dry after cleansing

Read more

Strong cleansers and hot water can strip your skin's natural oils, leaving it tight. Use a gentle cleanser, wash with lukewarm water, and moisturise while your skin is still slightly damp.

How to use sunscreen

Read more

Apply it as the last step of your morning routine, every day, even when it is cloudy. Use enough to cover your face and neck, and reapply if you are outdoors for long.

Do you really need a serum?

Read more

Not always. A cleanser, moisturiser and sunscreen cover the basics. Add a serum when you want to target one concern, like dark spots or dullness.

Questions

Good to know

How do I know my skin type?

Wash your face and wait an hour. Tight means dry, shiny all over means oily, shiny in the middle only means combination. Choose "Not sure" and we will start you gently.

Can I use all the products together?

Yes, they are designed to work as a routine. Introduce one at a time so you can see how your skin reacts.

How long before I see results?

Most routines need several weeks of steady use. Skin changes slowly, so be patient and consistent.
